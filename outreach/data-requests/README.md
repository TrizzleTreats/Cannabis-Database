# Data requests — 26 jurisdictions that publish no licensee list

Drafted 14 November 2026 by scripts/draft_data_requests.py. One letter per jurisdiction. **A person has to send these**: a records request needs a real requester and a reply address.

Each letter needs three things added before it goes out — the agency confirmed, the send-to address, and the state's public-records statute. None is guessed here; a wrong statute or a wrong recipient is worse than a blank.

**Read this before working through the table.** The agency column is extracted from this project's own audited report for each state, and it cannot distinguish a body that licenses today from one proposed in a bill. That matters here more than it would elsewhere: these are precisely the 26 jurisdictions with no licence data, and several run no programme at all. So treat a plausible-sounding "Cannabis Control Commission" as a lead to check, not a fact — and where a state licenses only hemp, expect the real recipient to be its agriculture or health department rather than any cannabis body.

**16 of 26 are verified against the agency's own site on 14 November 2026** — the agency, what it licenses, and the route to send to. A further 3 were checked and the result is recorded in the letter: a 403 to automated clients, a host missing from this project's allowlist, or an agency that turned out to run no cannabis programme.

23 of 26 name a cannabis, marijuana or hemp body in their own title, which is the only basis on which a recipient is filled in here. 3 are left blank for you to fill: ID, KS, WY. A blank is deliberate — a letter addressed to the wrong agency is worse than one addressed to none.

The ask is deliberately narrow and asks for nothing exempt — no personal contact details, no owners, no application materials, no patient or employee data. That is this project's own privacy rule, and it also makes the request cheap to fulfil and hard to refuse.
