What this file is. This is a separate fact-checking pass by an agent that did not write the original Mountain directory. Every original row was checked against the business's own website first (Categories 7–12 as thoroughly as 1–6), then against reliable public sources. The audit table below gives one verdict per original row. The rewritten report that follows keeps every Confirmed, Corrected and Unverified business with its details, applying only the corrections the audit found. Businesses marked Removed, Already listed (within this file), Licensed operator moved out, or Hemp licence only are not in the rewritten report; they are listed separately below so nothing silently disappears. Unverified does not mean closed or false — it means the audit could not confirm that fact on the business's own website or in an official lookup on 1 November 2026, and the row is kept flagged so a reader can check it. No bar, CPA, insurance-producer, contractor, security, hemp licence or hemp product registration number is published in this file unless the audit actually found it published — and in this pass, no such number was regulator-verified (see Method limits). Do not treat a self-reported number quoted in an audit note as a verified number.

Method limits — read before relying on this file

  • Own-site first, but not every fetch was fresh. Most own sites were opened directly on 1 November 2026. A few Cat 11 rows rest on search-indexed official-site pages after a browser-service 429 rate limit (Moonlight, PowerGrow, Libra, Monarch cross-ref, N2, Kinetic — crawl recency is noted in their audit rows), and a few Cat 8/11/12 rows rest on older crawls (Green Mountain Compliance 20 Sept 2026, Stericycle/WM 15 Feb 2026, Veracity 11 June 2026, COWGIRL SOSS pages 110–352 days old, Montana Pure indexed pages 254–345 days old). Those rows are not marked Confirmed on freshness alone.
  • Fetch failures are not closures. HTTP 403 (Colliers Denver, Grow Big Supply), HTTP 404 (Jankovic), 400 (Domonkos & Thorpe), and rate-limit blocks are reported as fetch failures, not as proof a business is dead. Only Moye White (defunct reporting), urban-gro (SEC-confirmed pivot) and Capitol Botanicals (live site, wrong category) are Removed, for the reasons given — not for a failed fetch.
  • Secretary of State registries were not systematically searched. The state entity registries (CO, UT, ID, MT, WY) are interactive portals and returned no direct record in the subagent text-fetch passes for most entries. Entity-registry status therefore remains open for every entry, especially Jankovic (dead site), Domonkos & Thorpe (dead site), Stack & Kottke (unreliable site) and Backen (no site). Existence in this audit means “own website / reliable public source shows it operating,” not “registry record seen.”
  • No credential was regulator-verified in this pass. No bar, CPA, insurance-producer, contractor, security, hemp licence or product registration number was confirmed in an official lookup (Colorado Supreme Court / state bars, CO DORA CPA, state insurance-producer lookups, MT DLI, UT DOPL/DPS-BCI, CO CDPHE/CDA, UT UDAF agbusiness.utah.gov, ID ISDA, MT/WY Departments of Agriculture, USDA hemp records). Every credential in this file is therefore Unverified unless it is explicitly labelled as a self-claim or third-party listing (e.g. Dude Abodes RRE-RBS-LIC-93060, Parks RRE-RBS-LIC-89332, Ajax ER40005471, First Colorado agent Keri Gage FA100093553, Safe and Sound California C7 #1039579, Sec-Ops WY CCDP-23-9, Colorado Air Tech H-3793, Monarch RCE-66836, LONG licences). Do not publish those numbers as verified. The one exception on type (not number) is Idaho Hemp Processing: ISDA confirms Handler type for 2025 and 2026; its number is not published on that list.
  • Licensed-operator clearance is “no evidence found,” not a certified clearance. The full Colorado MED licensed-facility database and Montana CCD licensee list were not directly queried in full, and Utah DHHS/UDAF lists were checked only for the Utah Cat 12 pharmacy flags (list checked: WholesomeCo, Beehive Farmacy, Zion Medicinals, Curaleaf, The Flower Shop, Dragonfly, Bloc Pharmacy, The Forest — none of the Utah Cat 12 names appears). A firm that advises licensees is NOT moved out merely for advising them. Only American Cannabis Company is moved out, on proven historical operator status, with current ownership expressly Unverified.
  • General-only rows are kept flagged, not upgraded. A general business (security, staffing, real estate, construction, cash, waste) is marked Unverified for directory inclusion unless its own site or a reliable public source names cannabis/hemp clients or a cannabis/hemp service. Business type alone was never treated as evidence. Dropping all general-only rows would cut the rewrite substantially (e.g. Wyoming Cat 9 would be empty, Montana Cat 6 would lose most rows) — the audit keeps them flagged instead, so the reader can see the gap.
  • Cross-directory overlap is flagged, not removed, for a stated reason. No other directory file was attached to this audit task. The auditor did check the Southwest audited directory and the National audited directory already on disk (see “Cross-directory overlap” below). Firms that appear there at a different office or service area (e.g. Vicente in Austin, HUB in Texas/New Mexico, ARCO in Arizona, ALK in Phoenix, GardaWorld in New Mexico, Stericycle/WM in Nevada, Dark Horse in Arizona, Blue Line Phoenix service, Vangst Arizona jobs) are NOT marked Already listed here, because the Mountain directory's scope is the Mountain office — deleting a Denver HQ firm because it also has an Austin or Phoenix office would erase the Mountain entry the directory exists to list. “Already listed” in this audit therefore means duplicate/cross-reference within this Mountain file only. If you are building one master list, apply the overlap list below as an additional dedup step.
