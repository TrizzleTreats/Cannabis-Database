Legal information, not legal advice

Everything on this site is published for information. It is not legal advice, it does not create a lawyer–client relationship, and it is not a substitute for advice from a lawyer licensed in your jurisdiction. Cannabis and hemp law changes often and differs by state, county and city, and a licence that was current when a regulator published its list may have lapsed since.

Where the data comes from

Official government sources only — state regulators, state statute and code sites, and federal agencies. News reports, blogs, law-firm articles and the commercial legal databases are not used as a source of truth. Every licence record carries the source it was read from, a link to it, and the date it was read; every count on this site carries the same.

What the counts do and do not mean

Issued, approved, licensed, pending renewal and operating are different measures. They are never added together here, and two figures published on different dates are never reconciled into one. A row count is not a licence count: one licence can cover several locations, and one location can appear under more than one licence.

An empty status is not an active licence. Some regulators publish no status at all, and those records say so rather than being presented as current.

What is deliberately not here

Personal contact details of individuals, and the owners named in regulators' files, are not published on this site. Directory entries that an audit could not verify are held back until someone checks them.

Corrections

If a record here disagrees with a regulator's own current list, the regulator is right. The source link on each record is the place to check.
