This page is meant to open with a clickable map of the United States, coloured by whether a state allows medical cannabis, adult use, both, or neither. That colour is a statement about the law, so it needs a sourced legal status for each of the 51 jurisdictions, quoted from that state's own statute and regulator rather than inferred from whether this project happens to hold licence data for it. Texas, for example, licenses no adult-use or broad medical operator but does run a limited medical programme — so colouring the map from licence coverage would misstate the law in several states. The research exists in the audited state reports; turning it into a status with a citation per state is the next job, and the map goes in once it is done.
