    Licences, by jurisdiction

    The regulators publish separately and on different dates, so these counts are not a single national figure and must not be added into one. 26 state regulators publish no licensee list at all — they answer one lookup at a time, which cannot be enumerated. Those states appear here anyway, because where a state runs no hemp plan of its own its producers are licensed federally and appear in the USDA's own list. A row marked Hemp Grower with a USDA source is a federal licence, not a state one.
