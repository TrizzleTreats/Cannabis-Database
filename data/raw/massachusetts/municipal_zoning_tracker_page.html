State & Local Government / Municipal Zoning Tracker

Municipal Zoning Tracker

The Municipal Zoning Tracker identifies zoning bylaws, ordinances, and local authorization statuses related to adult-use Marijuana Establishments for all 351 cities and towns in the Commonwealth. Municipalities may require additional local approvals, including Host Community Agreements or other municipal processes, before an applicant may submit an application for licensure to the Commission.

Applicants, licensees, and members of the public are encouraged to consult local officials in the municipality in which they intend to operate or receive services for the most current local information and processes.

The information provided on this webpage is intended as a general reference resource and does not constitute an official or comprehensive legal determination of local laws or municipal authorization status.

The adult-use delivery status column below reflects whether a municipality permits or has restricted adult-use marijuana delivery within its borders. Pursuant to Chapter 65 of the Acts of 2026, adult-use marijuana delivery is permitted statewide unless a municipality that does not authorize Marijuana Retailers has obtained a waiver from the Commission to prohibit delivery within the municipality. Municipalities can request a waiver by completing the Waiver Request Form. Delivery of medical marijuana by Medical Marijuana Establishments is permitted by law in all 351 cities and towns. Only licensed adult-use Delivery Operators authorized to conduct delivery operations may deliver adult-use marijuana or marijuana products to consumers.

The adult-use social consumption status column reflects whether a city or town has opted-in to host social consumption licensees within its borders. Municipalities that opt-in to allow Social Consumption Establishments are requested to complete the Social Consumption Opt-in Form.

While the Commission strives to ensure accurate and current information, municipal bylaws, ordinances, and policies may change over time. Municipalities are encouraged to review their listings and report any changes by contacting GovAffairs@CCCMass.com.

For the most up-to-date and official information, please contact individual municipalities directly.

Use the Where to Buy tool to find a licensed Marijuana Establishment or Medical Marijuana Establishment—including those that deliver cannabis to consumers and patients—near you.

Please note: When searching for a delivery business by address or zip code within the Where to Buy tool, results will show licensed entities physically located within proximity to that address or zip code. To find out if a delivery business delivers adult- or medical-use cannabis to your address, consumers and patients should consult the website of each business to see its service areas and product offerings.

    The municipality has enacted zoning ordinances or bylaws prohibiting all types of Marijuana Establishments
  • Ban in Place; Litigation Pending
    The municipality has enacted zoning ordinances or bylaws prohibiting all types of Marijuana Establishments that are under legal challenge
