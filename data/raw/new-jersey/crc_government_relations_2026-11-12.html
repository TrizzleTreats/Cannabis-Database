Government Relations

The New Jersey Cannabis Regulatory Commission (NJ-CRC) values the strong connections between state agencies and local municipal governments. Our Government Relations team plays a crucial role in building and maintaining these connections, serving as a key point of contact between the NJ-CRC and the state's legislators and those who lead and manage the townships across the state.

We work with New Jersey's municipalities to ensure they have the information and support they need on matters related to cannabis regulation. Here's how our Government Relations team helps:

  • Communication and Liaison: We act as the bridge between NJ-CRC and local municipalities, providing a direct line of communication for questions, concerns, and collaboration on cannabis-related issues.
  • Resources and Information: Our team provides comprehensive informational materials to legislators and municipal leaders, helping them understand cannabis laws and regulations. This support empowers them to make informed decisions for their communities.
  • Guidance and Support: We offer expert guidance on cannabis-related topics, assisting municipalities in navigating the complexities of regulations and compliance. Our goal is to ensure every municipality has the support they need to implement effective and efficient policies that will support the healthy, balanced, and socially equitable growth of New Jersey's cannabis market.
  • Collaboration and Transparency: We encourage open dialogue between NJ-CRC, state legislators, and local municipalities. This collaborative approach promotes transparency and strengthens the trust between state and local governments.

By fostering these relationships and providing valuable resources, we help create a strong regulatory framework for New Jersey's cannabis industry. If you're a legislator or municipal leader seeking support or information, contact us via email to learn more about how we can work together to ensure effective cannabis regulation across the state.
